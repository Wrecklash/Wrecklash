

# Hey, I'm Abhilash 👋

I've always been more interested in *why* people do things than *what* they do.

That's what pulled me into data — not the models or the pipelines, but the moment a pattern in the numbers explains something about human behaviour that wasn't obvious before. Why does a user churn? What makes content spread? Who's actually going to pay back a loan?

I spent 6 years chasing those questions at scale — at Sharechat (160M users), at ZET (Series A fintech), at Simpl (BNPL). Mostly from the product side, sometimes from the data side, always trying to make the insight actually change something.

The thing I'm most proud of? Building [STAD](https://wrecklash.in) from zero — a platform for esports players, no funding, no team, just figuring it out. 5K users, 6K Discord members, partnerships with S8ul and GodLike. That one taught me more than any job.

Now I'm at UTS Sydney doing a Masters in Data Science, learning to build the systems myself — not just define them for engineers.
